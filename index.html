Our Philosophy

A limited practice.
An unlimited standard.

Studio Prano creates upscale, premium environments that reflect considered taste and a refined way of living. We accept only a limited number of projects each year. Not as a constraint, but as a commitment. To quality, to presence, to the kind of attention that cannot be divided across too many rooms at once.

Every project is delivered on a turnkey basis using the finest German and Italian components, materials, and accessories. The site is handed over in ready-to-occupy condition. No loose ends. No excuses.

Join the Studio

Prano is a small, focused practice with a large point of view. If you are driven by craft, curiosity, and the kind of work that people remember. We would like to hear from you.

Open Positions

Three current openings, all based between Bengaluru and Berlin. We hire slowly and carefully. The team is the work.

Design
Mid-Senior Interior Designer
Full-time Bengaluru 3–7 yrs

We are looking for a confident, design-led Interior Designer to take ownership of projects from concept through delivery. You will work closely with Shreekara and lead client relationships, consultant coordination, and site supervision on real, meaningful projects.

Responsibilities
  • Lead design development across residential and commercial projects, brief to handover.
  • Produce detailed drawings, specifications, and material schedules in AutoCAD and SketchUp.
  • Present design concepts clearly and confidently at every stage.
  • Coordinate with suppliers, contractors, and specialist consultants.
  • Oversee site visits and ensure design intent is upheld through execution.
  • Mentor junior team members and contribute to a culture of design excellence.
What we are looking for
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Interior Design, Interior Architecture, or Architecture.
  • 3 to 7 years of studio experience, with a portfolio demonstrating quality across residential or commercial typologies.
  • Strong proficiency in AutoCAD, SketchUp, and Adobe Creative Suite.
  • Excellent communication skills and a genuine ability to hold a client's confidence.
  • Sharp eye for materiality, proportion, and the quality of a finished detail.
What we offer
  • Competitive compensation based on experience.
  • Direct involvement with studio leadership on interesting, varied projects.
  • A respectful, collaborative environment where your ideas are heard.
  • Opportunity for growth as the studio expands across India and Europe.
Communications
Social Media Manager
Full-time Bengaluru 2+ yrs

We need someone who understands design well enough to tell its story, and understands social media well enough to make that story travel. You will own Prano's digital presence and build an audience that reflects the calibre of the practice.

Responsibilities
  • Develop and manage a consistent content calendar across Instagram, LinkedIn, and other relevant platforms.
  • Conceptualise, capture, and edit photography and short-form video from projects and the studio.
  • Write sharp, on-brand copy, considered, minimal, and confident in tone.
  • Monitor platform analytics and evolve content strategy based on real data.
  • Coordinate with the design team to document ongoing and completed projects.
  • Manage community engagement with the professionalism the brand demands.
What we are looking for
  • 2 or more years managing social media for a design, architecture, lifestyle, or luxury brand.
  • Strong visual sensibility, you understand what good design looks like and how to frame it.
  • Proficiency with Instagram, LinkedIn, and scheduling platforms.
  • Clean, editorial writing in English.
  • Familiarity with Lightroom, Canva, or Adobe tools for basic content editing.
What we offer
  • Competitive compensation based on experience.
  • Creative autonomy to own and shape how Prano is seen in the world.
  • Access to beautiful project spaces across Bengaluru for content creation.
Design
Intern, Interior Design
Internship Bengaluru 3–6 months

Prano was built by someone who started early. This internship is structured around genuine project involvement, you will draw, research, model, and attend sites alongside the team. No filing. No coffee runs.

What you will do
  • Assist with drafting and drawing production in AutoCAD and SketchUp.
  • Support material research, mood boarding, and presentation preparation.
  • Attend client meetings and site visits where appropriate.
  • Learn how a professional design practice operates end to end.
  • Contribute fresh thinking to live projects, your ideas are welcome here.
Who should apply
  • Students or recent graduates of Interior Design, Interior Architecture, or Architecture.
  • Basic working knowledge of AutoCAD and SketchUp preferred.
  • Genuine curiosity about design, strong attention to detail, and the drive to learn.
  • Ability to commit to a minimum of 3 months.
What we offer
  • Paid internship with a structured, real-world learning experience.
  • Exposure to residential and commercial projects at various stages.
  • A letter of recommendation on successful completion.
  • Possibility of a full-time offer for exceptional performers.
